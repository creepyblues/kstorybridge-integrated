## What is Comps Navigator?

**Comps Navigator** is an AI-powered search tool that helps you find Korean titles similar to your favorite Hollywood shows and films. Instead of browsing through hundreds of titles, simply tell us what you're looking for using "comparable titles" (comps), and we'll find the perfect matches.

### Why Use Comps Navigator?

- 🎯 **Precise Matches**: AI analyzes story elements, themes, tone, and character types
- ⚡ **Fast Results**: Get 10-15 curated matches in 5-6 seconds
- 📊 **Detailed Explanations**: See exactly WHY each title matches your comps
- 💾 **Save Searches**: Bookmark your best searches for later reference
- 🔄 **Iterative Refinement**: Add text descriptions to fine-tune results

---

## How to Use Comps Navigator

### Step 1: Select Your Comps (1-3 titles)

Enter 1-3 comparable titles that represent what you're looking for:

**Examples**:
- **Single comp**: "Squid Game" → Find similar survival thrillers
- **Two comps**: "Stranger Things" + "Dark" → Find sci-fi mysteries
- **Three comps**: "Money Heist" + "Breaking Bad" + "Ozark" → Find crime dramas

**Tips**:
- Use well-known titles for best results
- Mix genres for unique combinations
- You can use TV shows, films, or both

### Step 2: Add Refinement (Optional)

Want to narrow your search? Add specific requirements:

**Examples**:
- "more comedic tone"
- "female lead character"
- "lower production budget"
- "set in modern times"
- "ensemble cast"

**Character Limit**: 500 characters

### Step 3: Find Matches

Click **"Find Matches"** and watch the AI work:

1. **Phase 1** (2-3 seconds): Finding semantic matches via vector search
2. **Phase 2** (2-3 seconds): Re-ranking with GPT-4 for accuracy

**Total Time**: ~5-6 seconds

### Step 4: Explore Results

You'll see 10-15 titles ranked by match score:

**Match Score**: 0-100% (higher = better match)
- 🟢 **85-100%**: Excellent match
- 🔵 **70-84%**: Good match
- 🟡 **50-69%**: Moderate match

**Each Result Shows**:
- Title poster image
- Match score badge
- Genre and tone tags
- Match explanation preview
- Individual comp alignment scores

### Step 5: View Details

Click any title card or "View Details" to see:

- **Full Synopsis**: Complete story description
- **Overall Match Explanation**: Why this title matches your comps
- **Comp Alignment Breakdown**: Individual scores for each comp
  - Alignment percentage (0-100%)
  - Specific reasons (character types, themes, tone, story structure)

**Example**:
```
Squid Game Alignment: 92%
• Survival horror with life-or-death stakes
• Ensemble cast with diverse backgrounds
• Social commentary on isolation and desperation

Black Mirror Alignment: 75%
• Dystopian setting with technology failure
• Psychological thriller elements
• Exploration of human nature under pressure
```

### Step 6: Save Your Searches

**Recent Searches**: Automatically saved (last 5 shown in sidebar)

**Bookmark a Search**:
1. Find a search you want to keep
2. Click the ⭐ star icon in the sidebar
3. Give it a name (e.g., "Squid Game + Parasite combo")
4. Access anytime from your bookmarks

**Load a Saved Search**:
- Click any search in the sidebar to reload it instantly
- Results are cached for fast viewing

## Tips for Best Results

### Finding the Right Comps

✅ **DO**:
- Use well-known mainstream titles
- Combine similar genres (thriller + thriller)
- Mix complementary genres (sci-fi + mystery)
- Be specific about what you like

❌ **DON'T**:
- Use obscure or very niche titles
- Combine completely unrelated genres
- Use more than 3 comps (limit enforced)

### Using Refinement Text

✅ **GOOD Examples**:
- "more comedic relief than Squid Game"
- "strong female protagonist"
- "suitable for streaming platforms"
- "episodic structure like anthology series"

❌ **POOR Examples**:
- "good" (too vague)
- "not boring" (negative descriptions less helpful)
- Very long paragraphs (keep it concise)

### Interpreting Match Scores

**High Scores (85-100%)**:
- Strong alignment across multiple dimensions
- Similar character archetypes and story structure
- Matching themes and tone

**Medium Scores (70-84%)**:
- Good alignment on key elements
- Some differences in execution or style
- Still worth considering

**Lower Scores (50-69%)**:
- Partial matches on some elements
- May lack key features you're looking for
- Review explanation to see if it's still relevant

## Understanding the Technology

### How It Works (Simplified)

1. **Your Input**: You provide 1-3 comp titles + optional refinement
2. **AI Analysis**: System generates "embeddings" (mathematical representations) of your comps
3. **Vector Search**: Finds 30 Korean titles with similar story DNA
4. **GPT-4 Re-Ranking**: AI expert ranks the top 20 by relevance to your specific combo
5. **Results**: You get the top 10-15 with detailed explanations

### What Makes It Accurate?

- **50+ Story Attributes**: Analyzes character types, themes, tone, genre, story structure, production style
- **Pitch Analytics Integration**: Uses professional pitch deck data when available
- **Combination Logic**: Understands how multiple comps relate (e.g., "X meets Y")
- **Context Awareness**: Considers your refinement text for nuanced matching

### Performance & Costs

- **Speed**: 5-6 seconds per search
- **Accuracy**: 85-90% match quality (based on testing)
- **Cost per Search**: ~$0.015 (API costs)
- **Monthly Limits**: None (available to all buyers)

## Troubleshooting

### No Results Found

**Possible Causes**:
- Comps are too specific or niche
- Combination is too unusual
- No Korean titles match the criteria

**Solutions**:
- Try more mainstream comps
- Use fewer comps (1-2 instead of 3)
- Remove or simplify refinement text
- Try related but different comps

### Low Match Scores (all below 70%)

**Possible Causes**:
- Your comp combination is very unique
- Refinement text is too restrictive
- Limited Korean titles in this exact niche

**Solutions**:
- Review match explanations (they may still be relevant)
- Broaden your search (fewer constraints)
- Try different comp combinations

### Search Taking Too Long

**Normal Behavior**:
- 5-6 seconds is expected
- Phase 1 (semantic search): 2-3 seconds
- Phase 2 (AI re-ranking): 2-3 seconds

**If Stuck**:
- Wait up to 10 seconds
- Refresh page if no response after 15 seconds
- Try again with simpler search
- Contact support if persistent

### Unexpected Results

**What to Check**:
1. Did you spell comp titles correctly?
2. Is your refinement text clear?
3. Are you combining compatible genres?

**Feedback**:
- We're constantly improving the AI
- Your feedback helps us get better
- Contact support with examples of poor matches

## FAQ

**Q: How many searches can I run?**
A: Unlimited! Available to all buyers (basic tier and above).

**Q: Can I search for non-Korean content?**
A: No, Comps Navigator only searches our Korean title library.

**Q: Why do some titles have higher scores than others?**
A: Match scores reflect how closely a title aligns with your comp combination across multiple story dimensions (themes, tone, characters, structure, etc.).

**Q: Can I save more than 5 searches?**
A: Recent searches show the last 5, but you can bookmark unlimited searches to keep them permanently.

**Q: What if I can't think of comps?**
A: Try our example searches on the main page, or browse the Title Library and use the AI Chat for discovery.

**Q: Do match scores guarantee I'll like the title?**
A: No - scores indicate similarity to your comps, but personal taste varies. Always review the full title details before making decisions.

**Q: Can I use Korean titles as comps?**
A: The system is optimized for Hollywood/global titles as comps. Using Korean titles may work but results may vary.

**Q: How often is the database updated?**
A: New titles are added regularly. Re-run saved searches periodically to discover newly added matches.
