## 1. Introduction

KStoryBridge is an AI-powered discovery platform that helps you find Korean intellectual property (IP) - webtoons, web novels, and other Korean content - perfect for your next project. Whether you're looking for the next big adaptation or researching trending Korean content, KStoryBridge makes discovery fast and effortless.

### Core Value Proposition

> **"Find Korean IP that fits your next show in under 60 seconds."**

No more endless scrolling through unfamiliar catalogs. Simply tell us what you're looking for - or show us a reference - and our AI finds matching Korean titles instantly.

### Who It's For

KStoryBridge is designed for anyone interested in discovering Korean content:
- Entertainment professionals seeking adaptation opportunities

## 3. Core AI Features

KStoryBridge offers three powerful AI-driven discovery tools, each designed for different search approaches.

### 3.1 Comps Navigator (Show-Based Discovery)

**What it does**: Find Korean titles that match the style, tone, and themes of shows you already love.

**Perfect for**: When you can say "I want something like Stranger Things" or "Find me Korean content similar to The Bear."

#### How to Use

1. **Enter your reference** - Type up to 3 movie or TV show titles
2. **Use autocomplete** - Select from suggested titles with posters and release years
3. **Click "Find Similar IP"** - Our AI analyzes your selections
4. **Review matches** - Get ranked Korean titles with match scores

#### What You Get

- **Match scores** (0-100) showing how closely each title aligns
- **8-dimensional analysis** covering:
  - Genre Blueprint
  - Tone & Mood
  - Character Types
  - Plot Structure
  - Setting & World
  - Themes
  - Target Audience
  - Format Style
- **AI explanations** for why each title was matched
- **Full title details** including genre, format, and key metrics

#### Example UX Flow

```
Input: "Stranger Things" + "Dark"
    ↓
AI Analysis (5-10 seconds)
    ↓
Results: 15 Korean titles ranked by match quality
    ↓
Each result shows:
  - Match score (e.g., "92/100 - Excellent Match")
  - Why it matches ("Supernatural mystery with ensemble teen cast")
  - Genre, format, popularity metrics
```

#### Tips for Best Results

- Combine 2-3 titles with similar themes for more precise matches
- Mix different genres to find unique combinations
- Use the refinement text to add specific requirements

---

### 3.2 Mandate Matcher (Brief-Based Discovery)

**What it does**: Match Korean titles to your specific requirements by describing what you're looking for.

**Perfect for**: When you have a production mandate, creative brief, or specific criteria in mind.

#### How to Use

1. **Describe your needs** - Write what you're looking for in plain language
2. **Be specific** - Include genre, tone, format, target audience, or themes
3. **Submit your brief** - Our AI processes your requirements
4. **Get matched titles** - Receive ranked results tailored to your brief

#### What You Get

- **Ranked matches** ordered by relevance to your brief
- **Match explanations** showing how each title fits your criteria
- **Key metadata** for quick evaluation
- **Save functionality** to build your shortlist

#### Example Briefs

| Brief | What It Finds |
|-------|---------------|
| "Romantic comedy for streaming, completed series" | Light-hearted romance webtoons with finished storylines |
| "Female-driven thriller with supernatural elements" | Action/thriller titles featuring strong female protagonists |
| "Coming-of-age drama suitable for teen audiences" | Youth-focused stories with relatable characters |
| "High-concept sci-fi with strong visual potential" | Visually distinctive sci-fi content |

#### Example UX Flow

```
Brief: "Looking for a revenge thriller with complex female lead"
    ↓
AI Processing (3-5 seconds)
    ↓
Results: Titles ranked by how well they match your criteria
    ↓
Each result includes relevance reasoning
```

### 3.3 AI Chatbot (Jinu)

**What it does**: Have a natural conversation to discover Korean content with the help of Jinu, your AI discovery assistant.

**Perfect for**: When you want to explore, ask questions, or refine your search through dialogue.

#### How to Use

1. **Start a conversation** - Ask Jinu anything about Korean content
2. **Get personalized recommendations** - Receive title suggestions with explanations
3. **Ask follow-up questions** - Dig deeper into specific titles or topics
4. **Click suggested queries** - Use AI-generated follow-up prompts
5. **View your history** - Return to previous conversations anytime

#### What You Get

- **Conversational responses** - Natural, helpful answers
- **Title cards** - Visual recommendations you can click to explore
- **Suggested follow-ups** - AI-generated questions to continue discovery
- **Conversation history** - Resume past sessions seamlessly

#### Example Conversations

**Exploring by genre:**
```
You: "Show me romance webtoons with strong female leads"
Jinu: "Here are some great options..." [Shows 6 title cards]
You: "Tell me more about the first one"
Jinu: "This title features..." [Detailed response]
```

**Finding specific content:**
```
You: "What Korean content is similar to Solo Leveling?"
Jinu: "If you enjoyed Solo Leveling, you'll love these..." [Recommendations]
You: "Which of these has the most action?"
Jinu: "For pure action intensity, I'd recommend..." [Refined answer]
```

**Discovering trending content:**
```
You: "What's trending right now in Korea?"
Jinu: "Currently trending titles include..." [Current picks]
```

#### Tips for Great Conversations

- Ask specific questions for better recommendations
- Use follow-up questions to narrow down results
- Mention titles you've enjoyed for personalized suggestions
- Ask about specific aspects (plot, characters, tone)

## 5. Title Detail Pages

When you find a title that interests you, click to see its full detail page with comprehensive information organized into tabs.

### Overview Tab

Your quick reference for essential information:

- **Title Image** - Visual representation
- **Korean & English Names** - Original and translated titles
- **Rating** - User ratings where available
- **Key Metadata** - Genre, format, tone
- **AI Insights** - AI-generated tagline or summary
- **Rights Information** - Availability status
- **Favorite Button** - Save to your collection

### Story Details Tab

Dive deeper into the narrative:

- **Synopsis** - Full story summary
- **Genre** - Complete genre classification
- **Tone** - Emotional style (dark, lighthearted, etc.)
- **Setting** - Time period and location
- **Character Overview** - Main character types
- **Themes** - Core narrative themes

### Platform Data Tab

See how the content performs:

- **Views** - Total readership numbers
- **Ratings** - Platform ratings
- **Episode Count** - Total chapters/episodes
- **Platform Links** - Where to read the original content
- **Publication Status** - Ongoing or completed

### Materials Tab (Pro Only)

Access business materials for serious evaluation:

- **Pitch Decks** - Professional presentation materials
- **Story Bibles** - Detailed story documentation
- **Additional Documents** - Scripts, outlines, press materials

### Credits Tab

Learn about the creators:

- **Story Author** - Original writer
- **Illustrator** - Artist (for webtoons)
- **Achievements** - Awards and recognition
- **Other Works** - Related titles by the same creator

## 7. Common Use Cases

### Finding Adaptation-Ready IP

**Goal**: Discover Korean content with strong adaptation potential

**Approach**:
1. Use **Comps Navigator** with successful adaptations as references
2. Filter by format (Film, TV Series)
3. Check **Materials tab** for available pitch decks
4. Save promising titles to your collection

### Researching Trending Content

**Goal**: Stay current with what's popular in Korea

**Approach**:
1. Visit **Trending** section for curated picks
2. Ask **Jinu**: "What's trending right now?"
3. Check **Platform Data** for viewership metrics
4. Compare multiple titles side by side

### Building a Title Shortlist

**Goal**: Create a focused list for evaluation

**Approach**:
1. Search using your preferred method
2. Click the **heart icon** on promising titles
3. Review your **Saved** collection
4. Access title details for deeper evaluation

**Goal**: Get professional materials for titles you're interested in (Pro feature)

**Approach**:
1. Find titles through any discovery method
2. Open the title detail page
3. Navigate to **Materials tab**
